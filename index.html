Stay/Switch

When your train gets delayed, Stay/Switch sends one alert. Swipe right to switch routes or swipe left to stay on your train.

  • Swipe right Switch to the 7 and save about 8 minutes
  • Swipe left Stay on the N. You won't get more alerts for this delay
  • ← / → keys Do the same thing from the keyboard

Drag the right half of the card to switch, or the left half to stay. Open in Figma ↗
